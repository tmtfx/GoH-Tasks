# Privacy Policy for Google on Haiku - Tasks

**Last updated:** October 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the **Google on Haiku - Tasks** ("the Application") handles your data.

## Overview
**Google on Haiku - Tasks** is an open-source native client for Haiku designed to interact directly with Google Tasks APIs. Your privacy is paramount, and this application is designed with data minimization in mind.

## Data Collection and Storage
* **Personal Data:** This application does not collect, track, transmit, process or store any personal information on any external third-party servers.
* **Google Account Data:** All tasks, lists, and account information retrieved via Google Tasks APIs are transmitted securely between your local machine and Google's official servers over HTTPS connections.
* **Authentication Credentials:** OAuth 2.0 tokens are stored locally on your machine to maintain your login session. They are never sent to the application developers or any third parties. These tokens are used by the Application to request access to your Google Tasks. This access is strictly limited to the permissions required to view, edit, and manage your tasks as requested by you within the Application.

## Third-Party Services
This application interacts exclusively with Google Cloud / Google Tasks API services to provide core functionality. Use of this application is subject to Google's Privacy Policy: [https://policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Open Source
Because **Google on Haiku - Tasks** is open-source software, you can inspect the source code in this repository to verify how your data and authentication tokens are handled.
